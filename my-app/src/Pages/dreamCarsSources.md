# My Dream Cars data

The 64 entries in `dreamCarsData.js` correspond one-to-one with the supplied cutouts. Model years select a representative specification for each pictured generation. The timeline displays researched used-price figures from `dreamCarsPricing.json`, sorted from low to high, with the uniform Average Used Price label. See [used-price research and sources](dreamCarsUsedPrices.md) for methodology, all 64 sources, limited-sale estimates, and limitations.

Original MSRP is retained only as reference metadata and does not determine the displayed price or order. The 1999 Japan-market S2000 has no U.S. model-year MSRP, so its USD MSRP field is null; Honda's Japanese launch price was ¥3,380,000 in Tokyo. The sources below cover original model specifications and historical MSRP. Electric boost and hybrid system outputs are identified in the vehicle notes. Engine descriptions use NA, TT, and Turbo labels.
